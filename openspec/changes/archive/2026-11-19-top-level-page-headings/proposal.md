## Why

Headings inside list items or quotes appear as page sections in the outline and the in-page table of contents.

## What Changes

- List only top-level headings in the page outline and the rendered `[toc]` block.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `page-editor`: Scope the outline to top-level headings.
- `markdown-rendering`: Scope the in-page table of contents to top-level headings.

## Impact

The page editor's outline and rendered `[toc]` block share the top-level heading selection.
